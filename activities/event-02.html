Math Club Internal Meeting — 80th Anniversary Exhibition Coordination

Date

2026-10-14

Time

10:30 AM – 11:00 AM

Venue

Mathematics Laboratory, Mahindodaya Block

Participants

Committee Members, Coordinators & Teacher Faculty

Event Overview & Full Details

The Math Club held a formal internal meeting on Wednesday, 14 October 2026 from 10:30 AM to 11:00 AM in the Mathematics Laboratory, Mahindodaya Block. The meeting commenced with the recitation of the Holy Qur'an, followed by an address by Mrs. NM. Zilmy (Co-Incharge) regarding the Math Club. Mr. MI. Nawas (Maths Coordinator) addressed the students and extended an invitation to participate in the school's 80th Anniversary Exhibition through the Math Club. President L. Mohamed Simam delivered a presentation introducing the Math Club and its vision and objectives. Secretary MJ. Abdullah delivered a motivational speech on stage fear and discussed ways to overcome it, encouraging members to develop their confidence and public-speaking skills. Mrs. SM. Saajitha Banu presented ideas for the development and activities of the Math Club, with club members sharing their own suggestions for the successful organisation of the exhibition. Coordinators were appointed to assist in organising the exhibition — 7 coordinators from Grade 10 and 2 from Grade 12 Physical Science for the Boys section, and 7 from Grade 12 Physical Science for the Girls section (16 total). The meeting concluded with Salawath.

Purpose & Objectives

To discuss and coordinate invitations for the exhibition organised as part of the school's 80th Anniversary Celebration.

Keynote Speaker / Resource Person

None (Internal meeting)

Teacher Supervision

Mrs. NM. Zilmy (Co-Incharge), Mr. MI. Nawas (Maths Coordinator), Mrs. SM. Saajitha Banu (Maths Teacher)

Organized By

The Infinity Creators MATH CLUB Committee

Key Outcomes

Exhibition coordinators appointed (16 total). Members equipped with roles and responsibilities for the 80th Anniversary Exhibition. Club vision presented. Motivational insight shared on overcoming stage fear.
